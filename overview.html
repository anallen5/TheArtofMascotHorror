What is Mascot Horror?

Mascot horror: a horror subgenre featuring a typically innocent or child-friendly mascot as the main antagonist/villain.

Typically we see these tropes in video games, the most obvious ones being:

  • Five Nights at Freddy's, a point-and-click horror game (2014)
  • Poppy Playtime, a survival horror and puzzle game (2021)
  • Bendy and the Ink Machine, an episodic survival horror game (2017)

History of Mascot Horror

In 2014, mascot horror was abruptly brought to the center of attention in the gaming community by Scott Cawthon’s indie game, Five Nights at Freddy’s.

After this major success, other game devs quickly tried capitalize on the subgenre (with varying degrees of success), beginning a 12-year history of mascot horror franchises.

  • Five Nights at Freddy's - 2014
  • Tattletail - 2016
  • Bendy and the Ink Machine - 2017
  • Hello Neighbor - 2017
  • Mr. Hopp's Playhouse - 2019
  • The Baby in Yellow - 2021
  • Poppy Playtime - 2021
  • Amanda the Adventurer - 2023
  • Garten of BanBan - 2023
  • My Friendly Neighborhood - 2023
  • Joyville - 2023
  • Indigo Park - 2024

Characteristics of Mascot Horror

The Mascots The Lore
Mascot cater to children, both in-game and in real life. Some are designed to be purposefully creepy, while others have lovable qualities that make the viewers overlook their murderous tendencies. The story told in mascot horror is usually focused around a corrupt company with nefarious purposes, taking advantage of the youth of children for ____. Very cut and paste, almost every single time.
The "Chapters" The Reputation
Horror games tend to label each part as a chapter, releasing every year or so. Some have 3 chapters, some have 5. Some are one-off games.

Five Nights at Freddy's (FNAF) follows no rules. It has 14 games and counting. 		Mascot horror, because of the “mascot” aspect, tends to get labeled as "childish" or "cringe" even when the underlying story is decent. This reputation also comes from content farms, which will take any opportunity to turn a good game into slop.
