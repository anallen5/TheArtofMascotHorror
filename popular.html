Henry Stein, a former artist at Joey Drew animation studios, returns to the abandoned studio upon request from his old friend, only to find the old mascots have been brought to life by the Ink Machine. He then has to try to escape, while encountering mascots both friendly and unfriendly.

One of the best mascot horror games artistically as well as story-wise, with a great variety of puzzles.

Synopsis:

  • Released: 2014
  • Chapters: 14/?

Five Nights at Freddy’s, the crown mascot horror game, centers around Michael Afton, the son of a child serial killer, who works the night shift at Freddy Fazbear’s Pizzeria, a place where fantasy and fun come to life! The four major animatronics, plus a secret fifth, come to life at night. Possessed by the souls of the murdered children, they terrorize Michael each night, becoming increasingly aggressive as they mistake him for their killer.

This set the standards for mascot horror. It is amazing story and gameplay-wise. I will take no further questions.
