Garten of BanBan features a daycare, in which the protagonist, an unnamed mother, searches the various abandoned levels of the Kindergarten. The various mascots, all horrifying yet silly characters, accompany, betray, and attack the protagonist repeatedly on her journey deeper into the conspiracies of the daycare.

Widely regarded as one of the worst mascot horror games, between the convoluted plot and lackluster animation. The entire vibe of the game is silly enough that there are still hundreds of fans.
