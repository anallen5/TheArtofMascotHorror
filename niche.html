Synopsis:

  • Released: 2023
  • Chapters: 1/1

Based on Sesame Street and Mr. Rogers’ Neighborhood, My Friendly Neighborhood follows Gordon O’Brian, who is sent from his job as a cableman to turn off the antennae at an abandoned television studio. He finds the studio overrun with violent sentient puppets and has to fight his way through while discovering the reason they have been abandoned there in the first place.

The game has 4 endings and a gameplay style that does not quite fit the category of mascot horror, but instead puts a unique spin on the subgenre.

Synopsis:

  • Released: 2024
  • Chapters: 1/?

Indigo Park, one of the most recent successes in mascot horror, follows an urban explorer sneaking into the abandoned theme park, Indigo Park. There, they are greeted by Rambley, the digital mascot, who guides them through the damaged park. Upon finding that the physical mascots, namely Mollie Macaw, have become violent, Rambley and the protagonist team up to restore the park and set things back to the way they were.

The first chapter set off a wave in the mascot horror community with its lovable mascot and great potential.
